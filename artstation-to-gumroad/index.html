0 Before you start (5 minutes)

Three facts that change what you do first. Tick them once you have acted on them — the deadline is close enough that a plan in your head is not a plan.

1 Connect and join the queue

Connecting an account is not the same as moving your store. The queue is the step people skip, and without it nothing migrates at all.

2 Set up getting paid — this also unlocks publishing

Payout details do not come over from ArtStation, and saving a payout method is what unlocks publishing on the Gumroad account. That is why this comes before you touch drafts: without it you can review them but not publish them.

3 Review every draft before publishing

Migrated products arrive in Gumroad as unpublished drafts. Nothing sells until you publish each one, and a draft you never publish never goes live. This is the step with the most hidden work, because the migration copies data but does not check it.

4 Categories that keep your ArtStation Store tab

Only Gumroad products in these categories show on your ArtStation profile. Type what you sell — “brush”, “texture”, “zbrush”, “photo”, “comic” — and see whether it is on the list.

5 What you do not keep

Ratings, reviews and sales history do not transfer. A product that had hundreds of reviews on ArtStation starts at zero social proof on Gumroad — and the old listing links no longer point at the product.

Two things people get wrong

1. Queuing is not publishing. Sellers who queue and stop there end up with a Store tab full of unpublished drafts on December 18. The queue moves your products into Gumroad as drafts; publishing each one is a separate action, and it needs a payout method first.

2. Category is the visibility switch. Same file, same price, same cover — wrong category and the product is invisible on ArtStation while still being on sale on Gumroad.

? Questions

When exactly does the ArtStation Marketplace close?

December 18, 2026 at 3:59 PM PT — December 19, 2026 at 08:59 KST. The Marketplace and Prints shop stop taking orders then, and the date is final. Missing it does not lose your products: digital migration stays open until February 28, 2027, but your listings sell nowhere in the gap.

Does connecting my Gumroad account migrate my products?

No. Connecting is the first half. You also have to press “Join the queue” — that is the trigger. After you queue, your ArtStation listings keep selling until December 18.

Why can't I publish the migrated drafts?

Published products need a payout method on the Gumroad account, and payout details are not copied over from ArtStation. Save a payout method in Gumroad's Payments settings and publishing unlocks. From India, a newly created account cannot set up bank payouts — PayPal is the method there, with the same unlocking effect.

Will my ArtStation Store tab still show my products?

Only if each Gumroad product carries a category from ArtStation's list (see the category table above). A product with no category, or with one outside the list, will not appear on your ArtStation Store tab. It still sells on Gumroad, but you lose the storefront that was sending buyers to it.

Do reviews, ratings or sales history come across?

No. A product with hundreds of reviews on ArtStation starts at zero on Gumroad. Save a screenshot or PDF of your best reviews, add a short “previously on ArtStation” line to the description, and point past buyers to the Gumroad version from your portfolio or a social post.

What if the product already exists in my Gumroad store?

The migration copies ArtStation products and does not merge them with listings you already had. Delete the imported draft and keep your original, so you do not end up with two pages selling the same file.

Can I connect more than one Gumroad store?

No. One Gumroad store per ArtStation account, and that store can be connected to only one ArtStation account. If you already sell on Gumroad, connect that store: its URLs, sales history and categories stay as they are.
