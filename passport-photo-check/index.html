    How the check works

    1 · Face landmarks

    A face-landmark model runs on your device in WebAssembly. It finds the crown, chin, eyes and the centre of the face. If the model cannot load, the head-line sliders let you place the crown and chin yourself — every other check still runs.

    2 · Compared with the spec

    Each rule is taken from the country's published photo guidance: head height as a share of the photo, eye-line band, centering, tilt, background colour and uniformity, and the pixels needed for 600 DPI at the print size.

    3 · Print-ready file

    Pro renders the crop at exactly 600 DPI — 1200×1200 px for a 2×2 in photo, 827×1063 px for 35×45 mm — plus a 4×6 in sheet of six copies with white gutters to cut along.

    Country presets

    Sizes and head-height ranges transcribed from each authority's published photo guidance (checked November 2026). Specs do change — the linked page is the authority, and the issuing office decides.

    Questions

    Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

    No. The image is read by your browser and never leaves the device. The page sends no request with your photo; the only network call is the optional license check when you enter a key.

    Does a green check mean my photo will be accepted?

    No, and no tool can promise that. The check compares your photo with the published numeric rules — head size, eye line, centering, tilt, background and resolution. Expression, glasses, retouching, paper quality and the officer's judgement are outside it. Treat a pass as “the measurable rules are met”.

    Can I print the 4×6 in sheet at a drugstore?

    Yes. The sheet is 4×6 in at 600 DPI with six copies and white gutters; order it as “actual size / no crop” and cut along the gutters. CVS, Walgreens, Costco and most labs print 4×6 for 15–30¢.

    Do you edit my photo (background removal, retouching)?

    No. The tool only crops and resamples. Canada, Korea and others reject altered photos, so there is no retouching, background replacement or skin smoothing.
