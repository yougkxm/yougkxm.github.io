What Etsy takes from a sale in 2026

An Etsy order carries three or four separate charges, and only one of them looks like a percentage of the item price. Read them together and the effective rate on a cheap order is much higher than 6.5%.

Always charged

  • $0.20 listing fee per item. It renews every four months, and again after a sale.
  • 6.5% transaction fee on the item price, the shipping the buyer pays and gift wrap.
  • 3% + $0.25 payment processing where Etsy Payments applies (US rate).

Only on some orders

  • Offsite Ads, 15% or 12% of the order total, on orders attributed to an offsite ad. Capped at $100 for any one order.
  • Shipping labels, VAT on fees, currency conversion where they apply — not modelled here.

What refunds change

  • Refunding through Shop Manager recalculates the transaction and processing fees on the reduced amount.
  • Cancelling the sale also credits the listing fee.
  • Postage you have already bought does not come back, and neither does the cost of the returned item.

Why a $25 item does not lose 6.5%

On a $25 item with no shipping charged, the charges are $0.20 + 6.5% of $25 ($1.63) + 3% + $0.25 ($1.00). That is $2.83, or 11.3% of the price, before you have paid for the product or the postage. On a $6 item the same fixed fees ($0.45 in total) are 7.5% of the price on their own, which is why cheap items look profitable in a spreadsheet and then do not pay out.

Reading the result

  • What reaches you is net profit for one order, after Etsy fees, product cost, postage and the share of orders that come back.
  • Break-even item price is the floor: the item price at which the order makes exactly nothing. Discount below it and every sale costs you money.
  • Target-margin price is the price that hits your margin goal. If your current price sits under it, the badge says how much more per sale you need.
  • Change any fee field to match your own Payment account statement — Etsy's rates differ by country and by payment method, and they change.

Questions

Are these Etsy's official 2026 fees?

They are the standard published US rates this page ships with as of November 2026: $0.20 listing per item, 6.5% transaction fee, 3% + $0.25 payment processing, and the Offsite Ads fee of 15% or 12% capped at $100 per order. Etsy sets rates by country and by payment method, and changes them, so every fee field on this page is editable: put in the numbers from your own Payment account statement. Etsy's Fees & Payments policy is the authority, not this page.

Does Etsy charge the transaction fee on the shipping I charge the buyer?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price, the shipping the buyer pays and any gift wrap, not just the item price. That is why the calculator has a separate field for shipping you charge: leaving it out makes the fee look smaller than it is on an order with paid shipping.

Is the Offsite Ads fee included?

Yes, when you pick a rate in the fee fields. Etsy charges 15% of the order total if the shop has always stayed under $10,000 in any consecutive 365 days and 12% once it has crossed that line, and the fee for one order never exceeds $100. The calculator applies the cap too. There is a separate page that models Offsite Ads in depth, including how the fee spreads over all your orders rather than only the attributed ones.

Does a refund give the fees back?

Partly. When you refund or cancel through Shop Manager, Etsy recalculates the transaction and payment processing fees on the adjusted amount, and cancelling also credits the listing fee. A refund you send outside Etsy, or a full refund Etsy covers itself, works differently and can leave the fees with you. This page models the common case: the 6.5% transaction and the percentage processing fee are charged only on orders that are kept, while the $0.20 listing fee and your postage are paid on every order, including the ones that come back. Check the fee lines on a refunded order in your Payment account before you rely on either assumption.

Do I need an account or an email address to use this?

No. The calculator is one page that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored on a server, there is no signup, and it keeps working offline once loaded.
