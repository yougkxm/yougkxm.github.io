    Etsy and Gumroad fees, 2026

    Fee schedules are the first thing sellers underestimate, and the second is returns. These are the standard rates this page ships with as of November 2026. They can differ by country, by shop and by payment method, so treat them as a starting point and put your own statement's numbers into the fee fields above.

    Etsy

    • $0.20 listing fee per listing (renews every four months, or after a sale)
    • 6.5% transaction fee on the sale price, including shipping charged to the buyer
    • 3% + $0.25 payment processing where Etsy Payments applies
    • 12% or 15% Offsite Ads fee on orders attributed to an offsite ad — optional to join, mandatory once you cross the platform's sales threshold
    • Returns do not return the listing fee, and card processing is generally not refunded

    Gumroad

    • Direct sales: 10% + $0.50 per sale on top of your own card processing at 2.9% + $0.30
    • Discover sales: a flat 30%, for buyers the marketplace brought you
    • There is no monthly fee, so a low-volume shop does not pay for idle months
    • Because Discover costs roughly three times the direct rate, the same product can be profitable on one channel and thin on the other
    • Digital products have no shipping and low return rates — the two numbers that decide most physical products barely move your digital margin

    Shopify and your own store

    • No per-sale platform percentage, but a subscription and often paid apps
    • Payment processing at roughly 2.9% + $0.30 depending on plan and country
    • Add app and subscription cost per order into the fixed per-order field to see your true cost
    • You carry the full cost of returns, which is where the margin usually disappears

    Break-even price, with returns built in

    Most calculators compute price − fees − product cost and stop. That number is optimistic in a specific, predictable way: it assumes every order is kept, that shipping is free, and that no ad budget is spent. Once you add a return rate, the break-even price moves up — sometimes by 20% or more on a cheap product with a 10% return rate, because fixed fees and outbound shipping are paid on orders that come back.

    Enter your price, product cost, shipping, return rate, return cost and ad spend, and this page solves the price where profit is exactly zero, plus the price your target margin needs. If your current price sits between the two, the badge says so instead of pretending the product is fine.

    How to read the numbers

    • Real profit per order is averaged across kept and refunded orders — so it is the number that actually reaches your account, not the best case.
    • Net margin is that profit divided by your listing price. A 30% margin on a $20 product is $6, not a comfortable cushion once ads and returns are in.
    • Break-even price is your floor. Any discount below it is paying customers to take your stock.
    • Price for target margin is the number to put on the listing, or the number to walk away from.

    Questions

    Are these the official Etsy and Gumroad fees for 2026?

    They are the standard published rates as of November 2026 that this calculator ships with. Rates change and vary by country, shop and payment method, so every fee field is editable — put in the numbers from your own statement. Confirm with the platform's own fee page before you price a product.

    Why does the calculator include return rate?

    A refund removes the sale price but not everything else: outbound shipping is gone, the return or restocking cost is yours, and the fixed part of platform and card fees usually is not returned. Spreading those across every order is what makes the profit figure match your bank account.

    What is a break-even price?

    The lowest price at which you make exactly zero profit after fees, product cost, shipping, returns and ads. Below it, every sale loses money. The target-margin price is usually the more useful number, because zero profit is not a business.

    Does Gumroad Discover really take 30%?

    Discover sales — buyers the marketplace brought you — carry a flat 30%. Direct sales to your own audience carry the lower direct rate. Treating them as separate presets is the point: the same product has a very different margin depending on where the buyer came from.

    Do I need an account or an email address?

    No. The page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and it keeps working once loaded, offline included.

    Can I use a currency other than US dollars?

    Yes. Choose the symbol in the form. No conversion is applied — enter every value in the same currency and the results follow.
