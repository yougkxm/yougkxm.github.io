What Shopify actually charges

Shopify's fee structure is different from Etsy's or Gumroad's: there is no marketplace percentage on your own store, and the cost arrives in two places — the card rate on each order and the subscription every month, whether or not you sell anything.

Per order

  • 2.9% + $0.30 for standard online cards on Basic with Shopify Payments (US, 2026).
  • 3.5% + $0.30 for premium cards, and +1% on international cards.
  • 2% third-party gateway surcharge on Basic if you do not use Shopify Payments — 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced.

Per month

  • Basic $39 a month, or $29 a month billed yearly (US list price).
  • Plus whatever your apps and theme cost, which is often more than the plan itself.
  • The store, checkout, SSL and hosting are inside that fee — there is no separate hosting bill.

What is not here

  • Chargebacks, currency conversion, shipping labels and discounts.
  • Taxes on products — collected from buyers and remitted, not a cost of the order.
  • Refunds: the card fee is not returned on a refund, so a refunded order costs you the processing and the postage twice over.

Why the plan fee belongs in the per-order math

$39 a month is $1.95 an order at 20 orders, $0.39 at 100 orders and $0.13 at 300. On a $40 order that is between 0.3% and 4.9% of the price, on top of the card fee — which is why a store that is "profitable per sale" can still lose money in its first months. The "orders to cover fixed cost" figure is the monthly order count at which the plan and apps pay for themselves.

When the next plan up pays

Grow is $105 a month with a 2.7% + $0.30 card rate. The 0.2 percentage points saved is $0.08 on a $40 order, so the $66 difference in plan price needs about 825 orders a month to break even on the card rate alone — before counting the extra features. Run your own order count and average order value through the calculator rather than comparing plan prices.

Questions

Does Shopify charge a transaction fee on every sale?

Not on your own store through Shopify Payments: there is no platform percentage, only the card rate and your plan fee. The fee people mean by a transaction fee appears only when you use an outside payment provider instead of Shopify Payments — 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced. The calculator has a field for it so you can see what routing around Shopify Payments actually costs.

Which card rate should I enter?

Your own statement's. On Basic in the US, Shopify lists 2.9% + $0.30 for standard online cards and 3.5% + $0.30 for premium cards, plus 1% on international cards. If a good share of your orders are premium or cross-border, enter a blended rate instead of the headline one — that is usually where the estimate goes wrong.

Why does the plan fee change the answer?

Because a fixed monthly cost divided by few orders is a real per-order cost. $39 a month over 20 orders is $1.95 an order; over 200 orders it is $0.20. The calculator shows that line separately and tells you how many orders a month the plan needs before it pays for itself.

Are apps and themes in the calculation?

Only if you put them in. Add your monthly app and theme subscriptions to the app cost field and they are spread over the same order count. Chargebacks, currency conversion, shipping labels, discounts and refunds are not modelled: each is a separate line on your payout report.

Do I need an account or an email address to use this?

No. Everything runs in your browser, nothing you type is sent anywhere, and the page keeps working offline once loaded.
