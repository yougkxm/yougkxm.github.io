Gumroad's two fee rates in 2026

Gumroad has no monthly fee. It takes a cut of each sale, and the cut depends on how the buyer found you — which is the one thing most fee comparisons leave out.

Direct: 10% + $0.50

  • Applies to sales through your Gumroad profile or a link you shared.
  • Card processing of 2.9% + $0.30 is charged on top, so the all-in cost is about 12.9% + $0.80.
  • Once paid direct sales in a calendar month reach $20,000, new direct sales that month pay 5% + $0.50. It resets monthly.

Discover: flat 30%

  • Applies to sales Gumroad attributes to its marketplace, where buyers browse instead of arriving from your link.
  • Processing is included in the 30%, so there is no second line.
  • Gumroad acts as merchant of record and handles sales tax either way.

What it means for a price

  • A $29 product keeps about $24.46 on a direct sale and $20.30 through Discover.
  • To keep $24.46 through Discover you would have to charge about $34.94 — 20% more.
  • On a $5 product, the fixed $0.80 is already 16% of the price before any percentage is charged.

Reading the result

  • Direct take-home is the number that decides your price for the audience you already have.
  • Discover take-home is what the same price leaves when a stranger finds you in the marketplace. Treat it as a different product, not a discount.
  • Break-even price is the floor for a direct sale; target-margin price is what your goal needs. On Discover both numbers are higher, and the "Discover price to match" figure is the one to compare against what the marketplace actually sells at.
  • Refunds are not modelled here: the fee treatment depends on the fee and the payment method, and the amount that actually left your balance is in the sales export. Use the main calculator when you want a return rate spread over every order.

Questions

Is 10% + $0.50 all Gumroad charges on a direct sale?

No. Gumroad's fee on a sale through your profile or a direct link is 10% + $0.50, and card processing of 2.9% + $0.30 is charged on top of that. Together they come to about 12.9% + $0.80 of the sale. The calculator has a checkbox for the card processing so you can see both figures; if you sell through a connected Stripe or PayPal account, that processor bills you directly and the same checkbox models it.

What is Discover, and why is it 30%?

Discover is Gumroad's own marketplace, where buyers browse products rather than arriving through your link. Gumroad charges a flat 30% on sales it attributes to Discover, and that 30% already includes payment processing. You are paying for a buyer you did not have to find. There is no monthly fee either way.

Is there a lower rate for high volume?

Yes. Once your paid direct sales in a calendar month reach $20,000, new direct sales in that same month are charged 5% + $0.50 instead of 10% + $0.50, with card processing still on top. It resets on the 1st of each month and a refund that drops you back under the threshold returns you to 10%. Discover stays at 30%. Tick the $20,000 box in the calculator to see the difference.

Why does a cheap product lose so much?

Because $0.50 from Gumroad and $0.30 from the card processor are fixed per sale, not percentages. On a $5 product that is $0.80 before any percentage is charged, so fees take close to 30% of the price. On a $100 product the same $0.80 is under 1%. That is the argument for a bundle or a higher-priced tier rather than a $5 entry product.

Do I need an account or an email address to use this?

No. The page runs entirely in your browser: nothing you type is sent anywhere, there is no signup, and it keeps working offline once loaded.
