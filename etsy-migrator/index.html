What happens in one pass

1

Tag and title audit

Every tag is measured against Etsy's 20-character limit and its 13 slots, duplicates and repeated words are counted, and the title is shown as searchers see it — cut at about 40 characters.

2

Shopify product CSV

One row per product, one per variant, one per photo, joined by a handle that stays unique across the file. Descriptions are escaped into HTML, not pasted raw.

3

Fee-parity price

The take-home on Etsy is calculated with the published fees, then solved backwards for the price that leaves the same amount on Shopify or Gumroad.

The Etsy export only contains listings that are currently for sale. Drafts, expired and sold-out listings are not in it — renew or re-export them first if you need them.

The calculation, in plain arithmetic

Etsy take-home at price P: P − 0.20 − 0.065P − (0.03P + 0.25) − offsite. Shopify take-home: Q − 0.029Q − 0.30 − plan/orders. Setting them equal and solving for Q is the price shown in the table, rounded up to the next cent so you are never short.

Example: a $38.00 sale on Etsy keeps $33.94 with no Offsite Ads. On Shopify Basic at 50 orders a month the same $33.94 needs $35.86; on Gumroad Direct it needs $38.27.

Etsy's payment processing is charged on the order total including tax and shipping, while the 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus shipping. This tool assumes no shipping and no sales tax in the price, which is the usual case for digital listings.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Active listings only. That is all Etsy's export contains.
  • Variants get the listing price. Etsy's export does not carry a separate price per variation, so every expanded variant starts at the base price and the product is worth a look after import.
  • Stock per variant is empty. Etsy gives one combined quantity for a listing; it is written on single-variant products only.
  • No product category. Etsy's export has no Shopify taxonomy category, and a wrong one changes tax handling. Set it in Shopify after import.
  • Currency is copied as-is. If your Shopify store currency differs from your Etsy shop currency, convert the prices before importing.
  • Images are linked, not uploaded. Shopify fetches each URL during the import; keep the Etsy listing live until it finishes.

Questions sellers ask

Does this upload my listings anywhere?

No. There is no server behind this page. The CSV is read with the browser's FileReader, and the download is built in memory. You can open the file with the network disconnected and everything still works.

Will Shopify accept the CSV it makes?

The columns are Shopify's own product import columns, in either the classic header names or the 2026 names, and every product row carries a unique handle. Import it as a draft first — that is what the default status is for — and check a few products before publishing.

Why is the Shopify price lower (or higher) than my Etsy price?

Because the fees differ. Shopify charges a card fee and a monthly plan instead of Etsy's listing, transaction and payment fees, so the price that leaves you the same amount moves. If you would rather keep your prices and accept a different margin, set prices to "Keep my Etsy price".

What about Offsite Ads?

An Offsite Ads fee only applies to orders attributed to an ad. Choose 15% or 12% if you want the repriced price to survive an attributed sale; leave it on None for an ordinary order. The fee is capped at $100 per order, and this tool applies that cap.

Can I migrate to Gumroad instead of Shopify?

This app writes a Shopify product CSV. The repricing table gives you the Gumroad Direct and Discover price to carry across, and the audit CSV works as the checklist for re-listing by hand.
